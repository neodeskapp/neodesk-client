# (src/tray_model.rs) both check their rules against these lines.

# Facts: status (ready, connecting, notReady), reason (the connect status
# reason), banned, enrollment, signal and said (the server's message) from
# the option neodesk-device-standing, stopped (connections paused) and
# noserver. Missing facts are empty, notReady or 0.
