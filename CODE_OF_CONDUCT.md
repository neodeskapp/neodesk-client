# Code of conduct

NeoDesk is built by people who want remote support to be simple and
safe. Treat everyone who takes part, in pull requests, on Discord and in
the Discussions, with respect.

- Be kind and patient, also when you disagree.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Don't share other people's private data, and don't help anyone get
  into a computer without its owner's permission.

The maintainers remove messages and contributions that break these
rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. If something
happened to you or you saw it, tell a maintainer privately on the
[NeoDesk Discord](https://discord.gg/nwERSawufr).
